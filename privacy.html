Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 23, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use the architecture contact form on our website, we collect your full name, work email address, company name, and details regarding your operational bottleneck. We do not collect cookies, behavioral trackers, or payment details through this site.

2. How Information is Used

The information submitted is exclusively used to evaluate your business automation request, prepare technical recommendations, and communicate directly with you about consulting engagements. We do not sell, lease, or distribute your contact details to third-party marketing services.

3. Third-Party Routing

Inbound contact forms are processed securely through FormSubmit.co and routed directly to our encrypted business mailbox hosted on Zoho. Data is stored strictly within authenticated business mail environments.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Inbound inquiry data is retained only as long as necessary to conduct project discovery. You may request the immediate deletion of your submitted contact details at any point by sending an email to our data contact.

5. Contact

For data inquiries or deletion requests, reach out directly to junaid@buildwithjunaid.com.
